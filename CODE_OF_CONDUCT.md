# Code of conduct

Arabati Wein exists to help families in and from Sudan find vehicles looted or
lost in the war. Many of the people who use it, report to it or contribute to
it have lost property, homes or relatives. This code applies to everyone who
takes part in the project: maintainers, contributors, partner organisations,
group administrators who send lists, and anyone who contacts the project.

It covers the GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews),
the project's Telegram bot and email, and any place where someone speaks on the
project's behalf.

## What we expect

* Treat people with respect, whatever their nationality, tribe, region,
  religion, political position, gender, age, disability or level of experience.
* Assume good faith, criticise the work and not the person, and accept
  correction without hostility.
* Write so that a newcomer, or someone reading in a second language, can follow.
* Protect the people the registry is about. Follow `docs/SAFEGUARDING.md` and
  `docs/PRIVACY.md` in everything you post or build.

## What is not acceptable

* Harassment, insults, threats, hate speech or incitement against any person
  or group, including about the war and its parties.
* Sexual language, imagery or unwelcome advances of any kind.
* Posting anyone's personal data: phone numbers, addresses, identity or
  registration documents, photographs of people, or a vehicle owner's details.
  This includes pasting them into an issue "as an example".
* Asking anyone for money, a fee, a "deposit" or a verification code, or using
  the project's name to do so. The registry is free, always.
* Contacting, naming or accusing a person who posted a vehicle or who may be
  holding one, or encouraging others to do so.
* Any sexual content involving, or any contact directed at, a minor. The
  project is not directed at children; if you learn that a child's data has
  reached it, report it and it will be removed.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately, not in a public issue:

* email the contact address on https://mycarsd.online/support.html, or
* message the Telegram bot named on the site.

Say what happened, where, and include a link or screenshot if you can. Reports
are read by the maintainers, kept confidential, and acknowledged within 72
hours. If a maintainer is the subject of the report, say so and it will be
handled by another administrator of the project.

On GitHub you can also block a user or report content directly to GitHub; this
code adds to GitHub's Community Guidelines and does not replace them.

## What the maintainers will do

Depending on how serious it is: ask for the content to be edited, remove it,
warn the person privately, suspend them from the project's spaces for a period,
or ban them permanently. Personal data is removed at once, whoever posted it,
and a record that is being used to target someone is taken out of the registry
and the next published build.

Decisions can be appealed once, in writing, to the same contact address.
