## Open Collective update (published 23 November, public, notify: no one)

Title: Second vehicle back with its owner

On 21 November 2026 the owner of a Hyundai Accent confirmed that he had collected his car. It is the second recovery confirmed by a registrant since the registry opened on 22 August, six days after the first.

He registered the chassis number as a standing watch on 16 November. The same number, against the same model, was already in a public list of found cars posted in a Facebook group, and he was sent the list that afternoon. Five days later he wrote back that he had received the car.

241 numbers are on watch today. Using the registry stays free: no owner is asked to pay to search, register a watch or receive a lead.
