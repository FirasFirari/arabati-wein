## Open Collective update (published 15 November, public, notify: no one)

Title: First vehicle back with its owner

On 15 November 2026 a rickshaw owner confirmed that he had collected his vehicle. It is the first recovery confirmed by a registrant since the registry opened on 22 August.

He had registered the last digits of the chassis as a standing watch on 11 November. On 14 November the same digits were found in a public list of found rickshaws posted in a Facebook group, and he was sent the list that night. The next evening he wrote back that he had received the rickshaw.

172 numbers are on watch today. Using the registry stays free: no owner is asked to pay to search, register a watch or receive a lead.
